## SUS SCORING INSTRUCTIONS

**Note for Researchers**: The SUS score is calculated as follows:

1. For odd-numbered items (1, 3, 5, 7, 9): Subtract 1 from the user's response.
2. For even-numbered items (2, 4, 6, 8, 10): Subtract the user's response from 5.
3. Sum all the adjusted scores.
4. Multiply the total by 2.5 to get the SUS score (ranges from 0 to 100).

**SUS Score Interpretation:**

-   **80-100**: Excellent (87th percentile and above)
-   **68-80**: Good (68th-87th percentile)
-   **51-67**: OK (51st-68th percentile)
-   **0-50**: Poor (Below 51st percentile)
