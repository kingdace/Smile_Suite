# SMILE SUITE: CLOUD-BASED DENTAL CLINIC AS A SERVICE
## EVALUATION QUESTIONNAIRE

**Project Title**: Smile Suite: Cloud-Based Dental Clinic as a Service  
**Evaluation Period**: Four-week evaluation period following system implementation  
**Evaluation Site**: Enhaynes Dental Clinic, Surigao  
**Total Participants**: 40 (15 clinic staff + 25 patients)

---

## INSTRUCTIONS

This questionnaire is designed to evaluate the effectiveness of Smile Suite: Cloud-Based Dental Clinic as a Service. Your responses will help us understand how well the system meets operational needs and identify areas for improvement.

**Please read each statement carefully and select the response that best reflects your experience with the system.**

**Likert Scale:**
- **1 = Strongly Disagree** - The statement is completely false or incorrect
- **2 = Disagree** - The statement is mostly false or incorrect
- **3 = Neutral** - You neither agree nor disagree, or you have no opinion
- **4 = Agree** - The statement is mostly true or correct
- **5 = Strongly Agree** - The statement is completely true or correct

**Please complete this questionnaire after using the system for at least two weeks to ensure familiarity with the platform.**
