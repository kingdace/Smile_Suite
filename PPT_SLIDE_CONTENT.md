## SLIDE 2: PROJECT OVERVIEW

**Content:**

This capstone project developed Smile Suite: Cloud-Based Dental Clinic as a Service, a multi-tenant SaaS platform for dental clinic management. The system was implemented and evaluated at Enhaynes Dental Clinic in Surigao, which served as the primary case study. The platform features a cloud-based architecture with complete data isolation per clinic, enabling multiple dental clinics to operate independently.

**Content:**

## SLIDE 13: SCOPE & LIMITATIONS

### **SCOPE OF THE SYSTEM:**

1. ✅ Multi-clinic workflows with independent registration
2. ✅ Online appointment booking with real-time validation
3. ✅ Email notifications for appointments
4. ✅ SMS notifications (confirmations, reminders)
5. ✅ Patient treatment history access
6. ✅ Medical records with dental chart integration
7. ✅ Inventory tracking with low-stock alerts
8. ✅ In-clinic cash payment processing
9. ✅ Financial summaries and dashboard analytics

### **LIMITATIONS:**

1. ❌ Web-based only (no native mobile apps)
2. ❌ Manual inventory entry (no barcode/RFID)
3. ❌ Cash payments only (no online payment gateways)
4. ❌ No staff scheduling/payroll features
5. ❌ No EHR system integration
6. ❌ Requires internet connection
